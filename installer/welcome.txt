Atelier
A sound design, measurement and DSP blueprint workbench.

Atelier is a VST3 audio effect. You build a sound by connecting
blocks (EQ, compression, saturation, tape, reverb...) between Input
and Output, then export a Blueprint: a measured, documented package
that describes the sound precisely enough to build a new plugin from.
Tested targets: FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper.

What gets installed
  - Atelier.vst3 into your VST3 folder
    (default: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3)
  - Setup notes and the DAW routing guide into
    C:\Program Files\R4N\Atelier
  - An uninstaller (Windows Settings > Apps > Atelier)

Nothing is installed into DAW program folders, and Atelier needs no
internet connection or account. Installing over an older version
replaces it.

Close your DAW before continuing.
