ATELIER by R4N
Understand sound. Shape character.

ATELIER is a VST3 audio effect for sound design, analog modelling,
measurement and DSP development. It runs inside your DAW
(tested targets: FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper).

What gets installed
  - ATELIER.vst3 into your VST3 folder
    (default: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3)
  - Setup notes and DAW routing guide into
    C:\Program Files\R4N\ATELIER
  - An uninstaller (Windows Settings > Apps > ATELIER by R4N)

Nothing is installed into DAW program folders, and ATELIER needs no
internet connection or account.

Close your DAW before continuing.
