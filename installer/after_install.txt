Atelier - installed

MAKE ATELIER APPEAR IN YOUR DAW
-------------------------------
FL Studio
  1. Options > Manage plugins.
  2. Make sure "C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3" is in the search paths.
  3. Click "Find installed plugins" (or "Find more plugins").
  4. Atelier appears under Effects (vendor: R4N). Star it to add it to the browser.

Cubase
  1. Restart Cubase - it rescans the standard VST3 folder at startup.
  2. If missing: Studio > VST Plug-in Manager > Rescan All.

Reaper
  1. Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST.
  2. Check the VST3 path includes "C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3".
  3. Click "Re-scan".

USING ATELIER (QUICK START)
---------------------------
- Insert Atelier on a mixer insert. It processes the track's audio.
- The Bridge (bottom) shows Input -> Output. Click a block in the
  palette to add it to the chain, or drag it onto a wire.
- Drag from a block's right port to another block's left port to make
  a parallel path. Parallel paths are summed.
- Click a block to see its controls on the right (SIMPLE / ADVANCED).
  Double-click EQ or Saturation for the large editor.
- A/B in the header compares with the unprocessed signal at matched
  loudness.
- EXPORT writes a Blueprint package to Documents\Atelier_Export.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- Atelier does not show up: rescan plugins (see above). Check that
  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Atelier.vst3 exists.
- Your DAW is 32-bit: Atelier is 64-bit (x64) only.
- Installed to a custom folder: add that folder to your DAW's VST3
  search paths.
- Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > Atelier > Uninstall.
