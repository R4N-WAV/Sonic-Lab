ATELIER by R4N - installed

MAKE ATELIER APPEAR IN YOUR DAW
-------------------------------
FL Studio
  1. Options > Manage plugins.
  2. Make sure "C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3" is in the search paths.
  3. Click "Find installed plugins" (or "Find more plugins").
  4. ATELIER appears under Effects (vendor: R4N). Star it to add it to the browser.

Cubase
  1. Restart Cubase - it rescans the standard VST3 folder at startup.
  2. If missing: Studio > VST Plug-in Manager > Rescan All.
  3. ATELIER appears under R4N in the effect inserts.

Reaper
  1. Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST.
  2. Check the VST3 path includes "C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3".
  3. Click "Re-scan" (or "Clear cache/re-scan").

USING ATELIER
-------------
- Insert ATELIER on a mixer/insert slot. By default it processes the
  track's audio (Source: DAW Input).
- Source: Generator plays test signals (sine, sweeps, noise...) through
  the chain; the harmonics panel then measures exactly.
- To analyse ANOTHER plugin: put that plugin before ATELIER and send the
  unprocessed signal to ATELIER's sidechain. Then choose
  "Reference plugin: sidechain -> main input" in the Analysis panel.
  See DAW_ROUTING.md in C:\Program Files\R4N\ATELIER for step-by-step
  routing in FL Studio, Cubase and Reaper.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- ATELIER does not show up: rescan plugins (see above). Check that
  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ATELIER.vst3 exists.
- Your DAW is 32-bit: ATELIER is 64-bit (x64) only.
- Installed to a custom folder: add that folder to your DAW's VST3
  search paths.
- "Sidechain: not connected" in the header: the DAW has not enabled
  ATELIER's sidechain bus. Follow DAW_ROUTING.md.
- Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > ATELIER by R4N > Uninstall.
